QuillAI — AI interaction redesign

Static mockup for the owner's audit notes 1–7. Design only; no product code. All colours are existing tokens from app/src/ui/tokens.css except the new --ai-* tokens listed in state 11. Renders of each state are in renders/. Header and rails follow prototypes/editor-chrome: series › book path, no save label, no counts, icon-only Continue, Help "?" above the gear.

01 · Continue streaming — AI ink, origin mark, writing head

Items 1 and 2. Fresh AI text is --ai-ink (blue), clearly not author ink. A pin stays at the caret where Continue was triggered; a soft block shows where text is arriving. The rail Continue control is Stop, at full opacity, with its shortcut. (Open in GUZ-1566: how Stop looks now that idle Continue is icon-only. The labelled Stop is kept here until that is decided.)

02 · Fade back to ink

Item 1. "Generated text in a distinct colour that slowly fades back to normal ink." Hold 1.5s after the run ends, then 10s to --ink. Today: --slate, 250ms hold, 1.8s (too close to ink, too quick). The last frame of this strip is live in a browser.

Applies to Continue, Edit replacement and Dialogue. fade: transition --ai-stream-color var(--ai-fade-duration) var(--ai-fade-ease) var(--ai-fade-hold) = 10s cubic-bezier(.4,0,.6,1) after 1.5s. author typing inside AI text is never tinted (unchanged). A new run during a fade restarts the hold for all still-tinted text. reload: tint does not persist. It is local presentation, not chapter content; after reload all text is ink. reduced motion: no transition. Tint holds 4s after the run ends, then switches to ink in one step. Settings "Tint AI text" off: plain ink throughout; origin mark and writing head still show.

03 · Continue control — placement and shortcut

Item 2. Recommendation: Continue stays at the top of the right rail. It is a fixed target that never covers prose, and the doc rule "one Continue control, no inline Continue button" stands. What changes: the Continue control is no longer muted with the rest of the rail. It is an icon button like the others; its name and shortcut are in the tooltip (drawn here as on hover) and in Help. The shortcut is the at-caret trigger; the origin mark is the at-caret feedback.
