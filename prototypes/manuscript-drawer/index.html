Manuscript drawer redesign

Static rebuild of the lost mockup for GUZ-1575..1579. One row size, one section pattern, add actions on the row, one Exports section. Nothing here is wired; hover and focus states are drawn.

1 · Default

Rows are 13px, one line, cut with an ellipsis. The kind sits small on the right. The "+ Chapter", "+ Piece" and "+ Universe" rows are gone. Every section uses the same heading: label, rule, action on the right. Exports is one collapsed section.

TreeRow: height 28px · text 13px/1.5 · indent 14px per level · kind 11px graphite, right-aligned SectionHeading: 11px/600, tracking .2em, slate · rule 1px --rule · 24px IconButton on the right Manuscript + adds a Universe. Series lore + adds a lore entry. Timeline is a plain row that opens the timeline.

2 · Add on the row

Add, rename and delete sit on the row. They replace the kind while the row is hovered, focused or touched. The universe row's + offers Container or Piece; a book's + adds a chapter with no menu. Hover is a neutral tint, not pink.

rest

Kind shown, no actions.

hover

Neutral tint. Actions replace the kind. Tooltip names the action.

keyboard focus

Focus ring on the row; Tab moves through +, rename, delete.

touch

No hover on touch: actions always show. Targets are 24px.

IconButton small: 24×24 target (the first mockup used 22) · 16px glyph · radius 7px · hover --hover-tint rgba(31,41,51,.06) Row + by kind: Universe → menu Container / Piece · Container → Book or Piece by its kind · Book → Chapter · Chapter and Piece → no +

4 · Exports open

One collapsible Exports section. Chapter offers Markdown, Text and Word. Book adds EPUB. Backup is the last row. This replaces the three "Download" buttons, the "Book:" link line and the backup button.

Exports heading uses the same SectionHeading; its action is the open/close chevron. Closed by default, state remembered. Compact button: height 24px · 12px/500 · radius 7px · border --rule. Chapter and Book rows are hidden when no chapter is open.
